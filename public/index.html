Your key, made and checked on your device

The keypair is created by your browser's built-in cryptography on your own hardware. Nothing is uploaded, and the page keeps working with your internet switched off.

Fast mode is the one exception: our server makes the key, locks it with a key only your browser holds, and deletes its copy. For wallets that will hold real money, keep Fast mode off.

  • Every match from the graphics card is re-derived with WebCrypto before you see it.
  • Fast mode results are opened in your browser and rejected if the key doesn't produce the promised address.
  • Nothing is stored. Close the tab and the key is gone, so save it first.

Questions

How do I use the key?

In Phantom or Solflare, choose Add wallet, then Import private key, and paste it. For the Solana CLI, use the downloaded keypair file. For a token, use the keypair as the mint when you create the token.

Why do some words take so much longer?

Each character adds about 58 times more work, or 29 times with any capitalization. Solana addresses also almost always start with 2–9 or A–H, so other first characters are 25 to 60 times rarer again. Short words and uppercase starts are fastest.

Why does Token address end in pump?

Many launchpads give token addresses a recognizable ending such as pump. The Token address tab fills that ending in and matches it exactly, so your mint looks native to the launchpad. You can change it.

What does it cost?

Searching on your own device is free. Fast mode uses our cloud GPU for longer words.

Can someone else get my key from the address?

No. The address comes from the key in one direction only, so a nice-looking address gives nobody a shortcut to your key.
