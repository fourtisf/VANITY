Terms of service

What the service does

{{SITE_NAME}} makes Solana keypairs whose address starts or ends with characters you choose. On your device, the search runs in your own browser. In Fast mode, it runs on our server and the result is sealed so that only your browser can open it.

Your key is your responsibility

  • Whoever holds the private key controls the wallet or token. Save it before you close the page. We keep no copy and cannot recover a lost key.
  • In Fast mode our server creates the key and holds it for a moment before sealing it to your browser and deleting it. For wallets that will hold significant funds, use the on-device search instead.
  • Every key is checked against its address before it is shown, but you should still test a new wallet with a small amount first.

Payments

Fast mode is paid in SOL from your own wallet before the search starts. The price appears on the page and again in your wallet before you approve it. Payments on the Solana blockchain cannot be reversed by us or by you; the refund policy below says when we send SOL back.

Privacy policy

On your device

On-device searches never leave your browser: the word you search for and the keys it makes stay on your computer or phone. The page stores a running Fast mode search (its id and the key that opens its result) in your browser, so a reload can pick the result up, and deletes it when the search ends. It also remembers which wallet you paid with last, and keeps a list of the addresses you made (the addresses only, never their keys) for the My addresses page, where you can clear it. If you press Show balances there, the page sends those addresses to our server, which asks a Solana RPC provider for their balances. There are no cookies, analytics, ads, or third-party scripts and fonts.

What our server receives in Fast mode

  • The characters you search for and a public encryption key made by your browser.
  • Your IP address, used to limit how many searches one visitor runs at a time.
  • Your payment, which is public on the Solana blockchain: the paying wallet address, the amount and the transaction id.

Our server logs record the time, search id, the characters searched for, the paying wallet and the transaction id. We keep them for support, refunds and accounting. Until a paid result reaches your browser, the server also saves the search request (never the key or the result) so that a restart does not lose it.

Others involved

To confirm payments, our server asks a Solana RPC provider about the transaction. Your wallet app is a separate service with its own privacy policy.

Refund policy

You get your SOL back when

  • a paid Fast mode search ends without a result, for example because it hit the time limit or the server failed;
  • we lose a paid search, or it never starts;
  • you paid twice for the same search.

Not refunded

  • A search that delivered a matching address.
  • A paid search you stopped yourself. The page asks you to confirm before it stops one.
  • A key you did not save before closing the page.

How to ask

Contact {{CONTACT}} within 30 days with the payment's transaction id. The page shows it when a paid search fails, and your wallet's history has it too. We send the same amount of SOL back to the wallet that paid, normally within 7 days.
